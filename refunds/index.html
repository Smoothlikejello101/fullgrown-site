Refunds

For FullGrown Plus, once paid plans start. Until then everything in FullGrown is free, and there's nothing to refund. FullGrown Plus is sold through Paddle, which takes the payment and sends your receipts as the seller, for Jason Russell, who makes FullGrown.

When you get your money back

FullGrown Plus starts with a 14-day trial of everything, with no card, so you can try it before you pay. After that:

  • Your first payment, monthly or yearly: a full refund if you ask within 7 days of it.
  • A yearly renewal: a full refund if you ask within 14 days of it.
  • A monthly renewal: no refund. You can cancel at any time, and you won't be charged again; FullGrown Plus stays on until the end of the month you paid for.
  • A billing mistake, such as being charged twice: always refunded.

Where the law where you live gives you longer to change your mind (in the UK and the EU, for example), you get the longer time.

How to ask

Write to hello@fullgrown.garden from the email you subscribed with, or use the link in your receipt from Paddle. For anything this page doesn't cover, write to us anyway: we'll look at it.

What a refund changes

The refund goes back the way you paid, and FullGrown Plus ends. Your garden stays: every plan still opens, prints and backs up.
