FullGrown

Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026. FullGrown is run by Jason Russell, Clark County, Arkansas ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Your plans live on your device. You can use FullGrown without an account, and then nothing you make leaves your device.
  • If you sign in, we keep a copy of your plans, photos and plants on our server so they sync between your devices.
  • No tracking, no analytics, and we don't sell your information. FullGrown has no ads. If it ever shows any, they'll be chosen by your general area or by what you're looking at in the app, never by following you around, and this policy will say so first.
  • You stay in control. You can download everything to a file, and delete your account from the app, at any time.

What FullGrown collects

On your device. Everything below is stored in your browser on that device. Unless you sign in, we never receive it.

  • Your plans: shapes, plant names, notes, statuses, dates and phases.
  • Background photos you add. The app redraws each photo before saving it, which leaves out the location and camera details phones store in photo files.
  • Your garden's ZIP code, if you enter one. To find its hardiness zone, frost dates and local plant records, the app downloads tables from our own site: the one for your ZIP's first three digits, and the ones for your state. So our host's logs can show those, but never your full ZIP.
  • Plants you add yourself ("My plants"), and your settings.
  • The app's last 30 error messages, if it hits any, so a support file can include them (see Feedback, below).
  • A random ID the app makes for each installation. It identifies the installation, not you.

If you create an account, we also hold:

  • Your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google also gives us your name, a link to your profile picture and your Google account ID.
  • Copies of your plans (ZIP code included), background photos and My plants, so they sync. Each plan notes the kind of device that last changed it, such as "Android phone", so your other devices can show it.
  • Your plan: trial, beta or paid, and its dates.
  • When your free trial starts, the installation's random ID and a one-way fingerprint of your email address, so a trial can't be started twice. The fingerprint is also kept when an account is deleted. It can't be turned back into the address, but it would match the address if someone already had it.

If you sign up for the beta, we keep what the sign-up form asks: your first name, email address, ZIP code or state, the devices you'd use, what you're planning and how you heard about FullGrown. We use it only to run the beta, and delete it when the beta ends. To stop floods of fake sign-ups, the form also keeps a scrambled copy of the internet address each sign-up came from, for an hour.

When you use the app or the website, the services that host them keep technical logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of each request. They keep them for security and to run the service; we don't use them to track you.

Feedback. Send feedback in the app opens an email to us with the app's version and your kind of device (such as "Android phone") already filled in. You see it, and can change it, before you send. If something isn't working, you can also save a support file there and attach it to your email. It holds your plans and My plants; the app's settings on that device; your device and browser, such as their kind, language, time zone and screen size; how the app's storage and sync stand; and the app's recent error messages. It never holds your sign-in or your email address, and holds background photos only if you tick them in. The app shows what's in it before you save it, and doesn't send it: it reaches us only if you attach it to an email.

Cookies. We don't use cookies for tracking. The app keeps your sign-in in your browser's storage.

Do Not Track. We don't track you across sites, so a browser's Do Not Track setting changes nothing here.

Payments. FullGrown doesn't take payments yet. Before it does, this policy will say what the payment service collects.

How we use it

We use your information only to run FullGrown:

  • to show and sync your plans, and keep your account and trial working;
  • to sign you in and send sign-in emails;
  • to answer you when you write to us;
  • to keep the service secure and fix problems.

We look at what's in your account only when you ask us to help with it, or when we must to fix a problem or keep the service secure.

We don't sell your information, build an advertising profile of you, use analytics or tracking tools, or use your plans and photos to train AI models. FullGrown shows no ads today. If it ever does, they'll be chosen by your general area (from the ZIP code you enter) or by what you're looking at, not by tracking you, and we'll update this policy before any appear. A link to a nursery may earn us a commission; the nursery sees your visit as it would any other.

Your choices

  • Use it without an account. Nothing you make leaves your device.
  • Take a copy. Projects → Backup → Back up all projects saves every plan and your own plants to one file you keep.
  • Delete. Delete any plan in the app. To delete your account and everything synced with it, use Projects → Account → Delete my account.
  • Sign out of one device with Projects → Account → Sign out. Its plans stay on it.
  • Ask us. Write to hello@fullgrown.garden to find out what we hold about you, get a copy, correct it or delete it. We'll answer within 30 days, wherever you live, whether or not your state's or country's law requires it.

Children

FullGrown isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database lets each account read only its own data, and photos sit in a private store. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you.

Changes to this policy

We'll post any change here with a new effective date. If a change matters to how your information is used, we'll tell signed-in users in the app before it takes effect.
