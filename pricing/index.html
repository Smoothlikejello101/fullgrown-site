Beta testers whose feedback helps shape FullGrown get their first year of FullGrown Plus free after launch, then the Heirloom price.

Your garden is yours

If you stop paying, nothing you made is lost. Every plan still opens, prints and backs up. The plan you edited most recently stays your free plan, which you can swap for another once a month, and the others can be opened but not changed. Sync stops, and the sun map turns off. After 14 months without a subscription, the copies of your extra plans kept on our server are deleted, with an email a month before. The copies on your devices stay.

Safety is never paid for

Warnings about plants that are poisonous to people or pets, invasive, or against your state's laws are free, always.
